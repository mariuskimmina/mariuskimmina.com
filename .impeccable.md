## Design Context

### Users
People arriving through the open social web, potential collaborators, and curious readers who want to understand Marius's work and interests without reading a conventional résumé site.

### Brand Personality
Warm, curious, and slightly handmade. Technically serious without feeling corporate; playful without becoming cute or noisy.

### Aesthetic Direction
A light, editorial "field notebook for the atmosphere": subtly green-tinted paper, inky type, turquoise and moss accents, loose orbital marks, and an activity constellation built from real ATProto records. Inspired by the intimacy and personality of small personal websites, but not a visual copy of any reference.

### Design Principles
1. Let real activity and writing tell the story before biography does.
2. Make protocol structure legible without turning the site into a dashboard.
3. Prefer rhythm, type, and small drawn details over generic card grids.
4. Treat mobile as a distinct reading context, with reachable navigation and horizontally scrollable dense data.
5. Keep every page static, fast, resilient, and useful without JavaScript.
